Bringing Ancestral Knowledge Forward

We are building a community where people come together in a good way to care for the land and all living beings. Rooted in traditional teachings, we live and practice ceremony as a daily relationship with the earth.

By weaving ancestral wisdom with modern tools, we create pathways for learning, accessibility, and transparency—ensuring the long-term care of the lands entrusted to us.

Honoring the Elements

Walking with respect and learning from the natural world.

Earth

Rooted in teachings, tending the soil, and walking with deep respect for the land and animals.

Wind

The breath of life, bringing unseen currents of creative expression and carrying seeds of hope.

Fire

Practicing ceremony and good fire, reawakening the ancestral knowledge of the past.

Water

Flowing in synergy, honoring the clarity and nourishment that sustains our shared work.

Tending the Land

Through stewardship, shared work, and teaching what we have learned, we walk with others in learning how to be in right relationship with the natural world. Our work takes place across many lands, including a 42.7-acre cultural easement held with accountability through community leadership.
