Bringing Ancestral Knowledge Forward

We are building a community where people come together in a good way to care for the land and all living beings. Rooted in traditional teachings, we live and practice ceremony as a daily relationship with the earth.

By weaving ancestral wisdom with modern tools, we create pathways for learning, accessibility, and transparency—ensuring the long-term care of the lands entrusted to us.

Walk in Harmony

We must learn from all four directions to find our center and walk in right relationship.

East Sweetgrass

Represents new beginnings, sunrise, spring, and childhood.

Focus on spiritual awakening, mental clarity, and the breath of life. Often associated with the eagle or the element of air.

The Gift: The ability to look ahead, seek vision, and plan for the future.

South Tobacco

Represents summer, youth, growth, and the peak of life.

Focus on emotion, passion, trust, and connection to the physical body. Associated with the element of fire and the buffalo.

The Gift: Warmth, passion, and the drive to take action on your intentions.

West Cedar

Represents autumn, adulthood, introspection, and the setting sun.

Focus on physical strength, maturity, inner reflection, and the realm of dreams. Associated with the element of water.

The Gift: The courage to look within, face the unknown, and find peace in silence.

North Sage

Represents winter, elders, wisdom, and the completion of the cycle of life.

Focus on spiritual wisdom, ancestors, and mental balance. Associated with the element of earth, snow, and the white buffalo.

The Gift: Deep gratitude, wisdom, and the knowledge passed down by ancestors.

Tending the Land

Through stewardship, shared work, and teaching what we have learned, we walk with others in learning how to be in right relationship with the natural world. Our work takes place across many lands, including a 42.7-acre cultural easement held with accountability through community leadership.
